# Company Scout Failure Log

Track every meaningful failure. This becomes your map of how the system breaks.

## How to use

After each evaluation run, record any failures below. A "failure" is anything where the system produced wrong, misleading, or missing output.

## Failure Types

- **WRONG_FACT** — stated something factually incorrect
- **MISSING_KEY** — failed to find important, publicly available information
- **HALLUCINATION** — fabricated a claim not supported by any source
- **WRONG_COMPANY** — resolved to the wrong company
- **BAD_SOURCE** — cited a source that doesn't support the claim
- **STALE_INFO** — used outdated information when newer info exists
- **FILLED_GAP** — made up data instead of saying "not established"
- **BAD_SCORE** — score clearly doesn't match the evidence
- **WRONG_PERSON** — attributed wrong role/title to a person
- **MISSED_NEGATIVE** — ignored public controversies or problems
