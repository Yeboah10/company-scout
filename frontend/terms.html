What this is

Company Scout is a free research tool. By using it, you agree to these terms.

The service

Company Scout produces AI-generated research briefs on companies. The briefs are based on publicly available information found through web search, processed by AI models. The tool is provided as-is, free of charge.

No guarantees

  • Not advice. Briefs are research shortcuts, not financial, legal, or investment advice. Do not make business decisions based solely on a Company Scout report.
  • Accuracy. The AI can make mistakes. Every claim cites its source so you can verify it yourself. Inferred contact details are guesses and are labelled as such.
  • Availability. The service runs on free infrastructure with daily limits. It may be slow, temporarily unavailable, or reach its daily quota. There is no uptime guarantee.

Your account

You can sign up with an email and password, or sign in with Google. You are responsible for keeping your credentials secure. One account per person.

Acceptable use

Do not use Company Scout to harass, stalk, or spam people whose contact details appear in a brief. Do not deliberately exhaust the daily quota to prevent others from using the tool. Do not attempt to access other users' accounts or data.

Shared reports

Share links are public. Anyone with the link can read the brief. You are responsible for who you share a link with.
