Company Scout

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What Company Scout is

Company Scout is a free research tool that produces evidence-backed briefs on African companies. It is operated by an individual developer, not a registered company.

What we collect

  • Account information. Your email address when you sign up or sign in with Google. We use it only to identify your session. We do not sell or share it with anyone.
  • Search queries. The company names you search for are sent to third-party search and AI services (Google Gemini, Tavily) to produce the research brief. We do not log your queries beyond what is needed to cache the report.
  • Cached reports. Finished briefs are cached so repeat lookups are instant. Cache entries expire after seven days.
  • No tracking or analytics. We do not use cookies for tracking, do not run analytics scripts, and do not collect browsing behaviour. The only cookie is your signed session token.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address from Google. We do not access your contacts, calendar, drive, or any other Google data. We request only the openid and email permissions.

Third-party services

Research queries are processed by Google Gemini (AI analysis) and Tavily (web search). These services receive the company name you searched for. Their own privacy policies apply to how they handle that data.

Shared reports

When you share a report link (/r/...), anyone with that link can read the brief without signing in. That is the point of sharing. Do not share a link if you do not want others to see the report.

Data storage

Data is stored on Render (hosting), Render Key Value (cache), and Render Postgres (accounts and reports). All are hosted in the United States. We do not transfer data to any other parties.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and data, email acquahyeboah01@gmail.com and we will remove it.
